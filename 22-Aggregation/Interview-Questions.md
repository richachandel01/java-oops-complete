# Aggregation - Interview Questions

## 1. What is aggregation?

Aggregation is a weak HAS-A relationship.

## 2. Is aggregation inheritance?

No.

## 3. Can the child exist independently?

Yes.

## 4. Example?

College and Student.

## 5. Aggregation vs composition?

Aggregation:

Child can exist independently.

Composition:

Child's lifecycle is strongly controlled by parent.
